# Code of Conduct

## Standard

Contributors are expected to communicate professionally, give constructive feedback, respect different perspectives, and keep discussions focused on the technical or documentation problem at hand.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Issues and pull requests that violate this standard may be closed or removed. Serious or repeated violations may result in the contributor being blocked from further participation.
